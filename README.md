Hi, I'm Noham. I'm 16, I live in France, and I'm the CEO of **[ParadoxalAI](https://paradoxalai.com)** and **[weareparis](https://weareparis.eu)**, the company behind it. I run both with my co-founder [Reel](https://github.com/Vyn042).

We don't build on top of someone else's AI. We train our own language models: our code, our training runs, our weights, on a gaming graphics card and whatever free GPUs we can get. Small models that look things up before they answer, and that run on the device of the person using them.

### What we're building

| | |
|---|---|
| **Vertigo** | A French assistant that runs on your phone or computer, even offline. It reads its sources before it answers, and every answer comes with a receipt: what was read, what was checked. Share the receipt and anyone can replay the check. |

### How we work

- **A lab, not opinions.** Every idea is an experiment with a written hypothesis, and nothing goes into a model until it has won twice, with two different random seeds. The failures are recorded like the wins.
- **Receipts everywhere.** An answer, a code change, an accounting finding: each one says what was verified and how.
- **On the device.** Our own TypeScript engine runs the models in the browser, on the phone and in the desktop app. Our PyTorch trainer runs on an RTX 4060 and on free cloud GPUs, session after session.
- **Our own code.** No borrowed model code and no borrowed weights.
